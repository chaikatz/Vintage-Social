Support

Help from a person

Write to support@vintagesocial.app. A person reads every message.

Members can also write from the app: Profile → Settings (the gear, top right) → Support.

Report or block

Every photograph and every member’s profile has a “…” menu with Report; to report a comment, press and hold it. Reports are read by people and acted on within 24 hours. To block a member, open their profile → “…” → Block member; Settings → Blocked members undoes it. A block also tells the admins, as a report about that member. In a message thread, the “…” at the top shows the other member’s profile and lets you report or block them.

Delete your account

Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm twice. Your profile, photographs, comments, likes, follows, tags, messages, application and share links are removed. The reports you filed stay, without your account attached, so that they can still be acted on. You do not need to write to us first.

If your application is still waiting or was not accepted, or your membership is suspended, the app opens on a screen that says so. Tap Delete my account there and confirm twice: your account is deleted in the same way. Or write to the address above from your account’s email and we will delete your account for you.
